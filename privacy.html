LED Board Privacy Policy

LED Board keeps your creations on your device while using privacy-aware advertising and configuration services for the free version.

  • On-device content: Boards, sequences, and background photos you choose are stored only on your device.
  • Advertising (Free tier): The free version displays ads powered by Google AdMob. If you upgrade to LED Board Pro, all ads are removed and ad SDK requests are disabled.
  • Consent & Tracking (UMP & ATT): Where required by law (such as the EEA/UK), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent preferences before requesting ads. After onboarding, iOS may ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission to deliver more relevant ads; you can decline and still use the app normally.
  • Configuration & Analytics: The app uses Firebase Remote Config to manage ad delivery parameters and Firebase Analytics to measure basic app events such as ad impressions, rewarded unlocks, and Pro paywall interactions.
  • Purchases: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app only receives whether Pro is active.
  • Notifications: Notifications are used only for reminders about boards you schedule, and only if you allow them.
